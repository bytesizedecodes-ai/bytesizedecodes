What is Artificial Intelligence?

Teaching machines to think — the branch of CS that builds smart computers

🤖

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of Computer Science concerned with building machines that are smart & intelligent — ones that can reason, learn, and decide like a human would.

Non-intelligent Machines

They follow instructions — nothing more

  • Do only what they're programmed to
  • Can't learn from new data on their own
  • Same input → same output, always
  • No understanding, no reasoning
Calculator Washing machine Traditional software

Intelligent Machines

They think, learn, and adapt

  • Learn from data & experience
  • Reason and make decisions
  • Understand language, images, speech
  • Improve without being reprogrammed
ChatGPT Face recognition Self-driving cars Recommendation systems
