What is Deep Learning?

The engine behind modern AI — multi-layer neural networks

🕸️

Deep Learning is a subfield of Machine Learning that uses Artificial Neural Networks — many layers of them — to learn patterns directly from data.

Why "deep"? And why does it matter?

🧱 "Deep" = many hidden layers

A traditional network has one or two layers. A deep network stacks many — each layer learning something more abstract than the one before.

  • Early layers spot edges & colours
  • Middle layers spot shapes & parts
  • Later layers spot full objects

🚀 Why it wins on hard tasks

Deep networks learn features straight from raw data — no manual feature engineering. That's why they dominate the hard stuff.

  • Image recognition (faces, medical scans)
  • Language — ChatGPT, translation
  • Speech → text
  • Self-driving perception
