## Build order — one course at a time, each fully wired

### Course 1: `ai-security-foundations` (beginner, free, 10 lessons × ~5 min)
1. Load the `infographics-interactive-beginner_v1` skill and follow it for all authoring (story-mountain, no math/code, 3-choice MCQs, full jargon gate with same-sentence translations).
2. Create 10 infographic components in `components/infographic/` (compact ClipOnLenses.tsx style, shared primitives/Scene/Stagger, theme-safe tokens, one accent per scene):
   `WhyAIGetsAttacked.tsx`, `OwaspLlmTopTen.tsx`, `PromptInjection.tsx`, `Jailbreaks.tsx`, `DataLeakage.tsx`, `GuardrailsBasics.tsx`, `SupplyChainRisk.tsx`, `OverpermissionedAgents.tsx`, `WhenSecurityKillsTheProduct.tsx`, `SecurityCapstone.tsx`
3. Append the Course object to `COURSES` (slug/title/subtitle/description/level/domain/access + 10 Lessons with concept briefs anchor-first, prose ≥2 paragraphs, exactly 2 scenario MCQs each, ~5 min, diagram where it earns its place).
4. Register components in `components/infographic/index.tsx` under `// AI Security courses` comments.
5. New SkillIds in `content/types.ts` + definitions in `content/skills.ts` (each <1024 chars): `ai-attack-surface`, `owasp-llm-top-ten`, `prompt-injection`, `jailbreak-techniques`, `llm-data-leakage`, `guardrail-basics`, `model-supply-chain`, `agent-permissions`, `security-ux-tradeoffs`.
