## Plan: Build intermediate + advanced AI Observability courses together

### Evaluation: what I adopted vs. added
**Adopted from the other agent's plan** (pedagogically superior): a single evolving **narrative case** per course — a customer-support RAG assistant incident (intermediate) and a multi-agent financial-research system across regions/providers (advanced); explicit format/time targets; "required interactive beats" + "expert activities"; phased delivery; acceptance criteria; "risk as lifecycle, not checklist"; "tools/vendor comparative & optional."
**Added** (gaps in both plans): (1) a real **security & privacy spine** anchored on the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs (2025) — prompt injection, insecure output handling, sensitive-info disclosure, excessive agency, supply chain, model DoS — plus attack-detection-in-telemetry (model extraction, membership inference, data exfiltration), guardrails (with evasion honesty), and privacy-engineering (residency/tenant-isolation/RBAC/DP); (2) a named **governance-frameworks** lesson (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, SR 11-7); (3) concrete current-tech anchors folded into the narrative (OTel GenAI vs OpenInference, G-Eval, judge bias, RAGAS, semantic caching/model routing, MCP); (4) concrete `SkillId`s; (5) exact file/line touch-points; (6) a paid-gating honesty caveat.

### Design principles
- Reuse `domain: "AI Observability"` (no new domain). Levels `"intermediate"` + `"expert"` (the platform's `CourseLevel` enum uses `expert`, not "advanced").
- Intermediate = **PIC** framework (Scenario→Predict→Observe→Explain-via-case→Transfer), task-framed, code-welcome, 2–3 Pyodide exercises. Expert = **CER** (Problem-first→Diagnose→Claim/Evidence/Reasoning→Counter-examples→What/So-What/Now-What), sparse/`open & minimal` interaction, full precision, real code/edge cases.
- Every interactive beat passes the skill's **three gates** (restraint/affordance/discoverability). Expert widgets are diagnostic, not tutorial-y.
- Security/privacy/governance is a **through-line**, not one lesson — marked 🔒 below.
