# Phase 2 — Active AI Foundations

Parent: [`../05-product-execution-roadmap.md`](../05-product-execution-roadmap.md)  
Duration: 4–6 weeks at one to two authored units per week

## Build first reusable interactions

1. Prediction card
2. Sort/classify
3. Sequence builder
4. Slider sandbox
5. Explain-back
6. Decision case

## Finish the AI Foundations course

Outcome:

- The learner can explain core AI categories, identify appropriate use cases,
  recognize common failure modes, and make a responsible use decision.

Final artifact:

- Analyze one real AI product and publish:
  - What it does
  - What data it likely uses
  - Where it can fail
  - How a human should oversee it

## Learning validation

Use:

- Pre-course diagnostic
- Immediate post-unit questions
- Seven-day delayed transfer test
- Artifact rubric

## Exit criteria

- Median course continuation after the first unit exceeds 50%.
- Delayed-test accuracy improves meaningfully over the diagnostic.
- At least 30% of starters finish the artifact.
- Testers identify the active interactions—not XP—as a reason to return.
