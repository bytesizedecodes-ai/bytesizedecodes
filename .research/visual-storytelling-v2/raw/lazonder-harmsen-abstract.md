# Lazonder & Harmsen (2016), Meta-Analysis of Inquiry-Based Learning: Effects of Guidance, RER 86(3)
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/0034654315627366 (abstract, fetched via webReader)

72 studies. Overall effects of guidance in inquiry learning:
- learning activities d = 0.66 (95% CI [0.44, 0.88])
- performance success d = 0.71 (95% CI [0.52, 0.90])
- learning outcomes d = 0.50 (95% CI [0.37, 0.62])
Type of guidance moderated effects on performance success but not other outcomes.
"Inquiry-based learning can be more effective than other, more expository instructional approaches as long as students are supported adequately."
Cites Alfieri et al. 2011 (discovery-based instruction), Kirschner et al. 2006 (minimal guidance), De Jong & Lazonder 2014 (guided discovery principle in Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning).
