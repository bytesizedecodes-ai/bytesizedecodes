# Richter, Scheiter & Eitel (2016), Signaling text-picture relations in multimedia learning: A comprehensive meta-analysis, Educational Research Review 17
Landing: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1747938X15000664 | DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2015.12.003

Meta-analysis of 103 comparisons (N = 12,775). Pooled effects of signaling (e.g., arrows, highlighting, coloring, deictic references, emphasizing headings):
- Comprehension (retention + transfer): g = 0.43, 95% CI [0.34, 0.51]
- Retention: g = 0.40; Transfer: g = 0.47 (transfer from signaled / non-signaled distinction: stronger when signals spanned text AND pictures vs within one representation only)
- Effects robust across student levels, domains (STEM dominant), pacing, presentation format.
Caveat: mostly short-term outcomes; few studies measured transfer in delayed tests.
